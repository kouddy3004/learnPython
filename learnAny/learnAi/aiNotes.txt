Magic Prompt Formula (RAID Framework)

		Role: Define AI’s persona (e.g., “experienced email copywriter from Ogilvy, Uber, Google”)
		Ask: Specific task request
		Instruction: Detailed context about audience, tone, requirements
		Data: Include relevant information, reviews, examples
			Demonstrated with email writing: basic prompt got 6/10 result, RAID formula achieved 8.5/10
